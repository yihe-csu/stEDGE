# Dataset availability

All datasets used in the stEDGE study are publicly available spatial transcriptomics datasets obtained from previously published studies or public data repositories. The datasets include mouse embryo, human intestinal Crohn’s disease, human breast cancer, mouse brain, and human fibrotic lung spatial transcriptomics data.

The mouse embryo datasets used for developmental tissue analysis (Data 1–9) were obtained from the MOSTA atlas:

- Chen et al., *Cell*, 2022
- China National GeneBank Database (CNGBdb):  
  https://db.cngb.org/stomics/mosta/

The human intestinal datasets from Crohn’s disease patients (Data 10–11) were obtained from Kong et al.:

- Kong, L. et al. *Single-cell and spatial transcriptomics of stricturing Crohn’s disease highlights a fibrosis-associated network*. **Nature Genetics** 57, 1742–1753 (2025).  
  Article: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-025-02225-y
- Spatial transcriptomics data and analysis code:  
  https://zenodo.org/records/14509802

The human breast cancer datasets (Data 12 and Data 16) were obtained from the 10x Genomics public spatial gene expression resources:

- 10x Genomics public datasets:  
  https://support.10xgenomics.com/spatial-gene-expression/datasets

The mouse brain datasets, including hippocampus and cerebellum sections (Data 13–14), were obtained from the Slide-seqV2 study:

- Stickels et al., *Nature Biotechnology*, 2021
- Broad Institute Single-Cell Portal:  
  https://singlecell.broadinstitute.org/single_cell/study/SCP815

The Xenium datasets of human fibrotic lung tissue from systemic sclerosis-associated interstitial lung disease (SSc-ILD) (Data 15 and Data 17), including sections SSc_1_1_2 and SSc_1_1_1, were obtained from Markov et al.:

- NCBI GEO accession: GSE303048  
- NCBI GEO:  
  https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE303048

Please refer to the stEDGE manuscript Methods section and Supplementary Tables 1–2 for detailed dataset descriptions, preprocessing procedures, and benchmark settings.
